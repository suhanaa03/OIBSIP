## Project Overview

This project performs Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) on a retail sales dataset using Python. The objective is to analyze sales trends, customer demographics, and product performance to uncover meaningful business insights and support data-driven decision-making.

---

## Objectives

- Perform initial data inspection
- Analyze descriptive statistics
- Study monthly and quarterly sales trends
- Analyze customer demographics
- Identify top-performing product categories
- Visualize correlations between numerical variables
- Generate business recommendations based on the analysis

## Key Business Insights

- Sales fluctuate throughout the year, indicating seasonal demand.
- Some product categories generate significantly higher revenue than others.
- Customer purchasing behavior varies across different age groups.
- Price per Unit has a strong positive correlation with Total Amount.

---

## Business Recommendations

1. Increase inventory during high-demand months.
2. Focus marketing campaigns on high-performing product categories.
3. Introduce promotional offers during low-sales periods.
4. Use customer demographic insights to create targeted marketing campaigns.
5. Monitor pricing strategies to maximize revenue.
